Every child
deserves a chance.

UCCF provides the critical non-medical support that keeps children in treatment at the Uganda Cancer Institute. Without transport, nutrition, medications, and psychosocial care, families abandon care — not because treatment is unavailable, but because they cannot stay in it.

The Challenge

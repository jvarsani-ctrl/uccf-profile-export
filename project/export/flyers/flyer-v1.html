Uganda Child Cancer Foundation (UCCF) is Uganda's first civil society organisation dedicated exclusively to childhood cancer — founded in 2006 by doctors, nurses, and parents at the Uganda Cancer Institute. We provide the life-enabling support that keeps children in treatment and gives them a fighting chance at survival.

Our Four Programmes

01
Patient Support
Diagnostics, cancer medication, nutrition, transport, psychosocial counselling, WASH, and caretaker support — enabling 1,000+ children to complete treatment annually.
02
Education & Awareness
3C Programme in 200+ schools, community outreach, health worker training, and national media campaigns to drive early detection and reduce stigma.
03
Research
National paediatric cancer registry, treatment abandonment research, and representation at ASCO, St. Jude Global Alliance, and the 4th Global NCD Forum.
04
Resource Mobilisation
Childhood Cancer Colour Run (5,000–6,000 participants, May 2026), Guardian Angel monthly giving, Annual Dinner, in-kind partnerships, and policy advocacy.

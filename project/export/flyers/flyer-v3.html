Founded in 2006 at the Uganda Cancer Institute, UCCF ensures that no child abandons cancer treatment because of poverty, distance, or lack of support — providing direct patient care to 1,000+ children each year.

Our Four Programmes

01 — Patient Support
Medical + Non-Medical Care
Diagnostics, medication, nutrition, transport, psychosocial counselling, WASH, and caretaker support for children at UCI.
02 — Education & Awareness
3C Schools + Outreach
200+ school clubs, community outreach, health worker training, and national media to drive early detection.
03 — Research
Data That Saves Lives
National cancer registry, treatment abandonment research, and representation at ASCO, St. Jude Alliance, and global NCD forums.
04 — Resource Mobilisation
Colour Run & More
Annual Colour Run (5,000–6,000 participants), Guardian Angel monthly giving, corporate partnerships, and policy advocacy.
